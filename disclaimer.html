Medical & Clinical Disclaimer

Important information regarding our role as a medical travel facilitator and non-provider of direct medical treatment.

Important Health & Safety Notice

If you are experiencing an acute, life-threatening medical emergency, please do not use this website or wait for an international consultation. Seek immediate emergency medical attention in your local area.

1. Facilitation Role Only

MEDMOST is an independent medical travel coordinator and facilitator, NOT a hospital, medical clinic, physician group, or licensed healthcare provider. We do not employ doctors to provide clinical diagnoses, perform surgical procedures, or administer medications.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Communicating with MEDMOST, filling out consultation forms, or speaking with our coordinators does NOT establish a doctor-patient relationship. A legal and clinical doctor-patient relationship is formed solely between the patient and the licensed medical practitioners at the accredited hospital in India upon formal in-person examination and signed medical consent.

3. No Guaranteed Clinical Outcomes

Medicine and surgery inherently involve risks, variability in human biology, and unforeseen complications. MEDMOST does not guarantee, promise, or warrant any specific medical outcome, surgical cure, or recovery speed. Any historical success rates or procedural statistics mentioned are general literature benchmarks and not specific promises for individual patients.

4. First Version Prototype Disclosure

This first release of the MEDMOST web presence represents a design prototype. Doctor listings, hospital listings, testimonials, and sample ratings shown on the prototype are demo placeholders created to review layout and structure. Hospital partnerships and consultant availability are verified with each individual patient inquiry.

5. Independent Second Opinions

MEDMOST strongly encourages all patients to discuss planned medical travel and overseas surgical recommendations with their primary home physician or trusted local medical specialists before committing to travel.
