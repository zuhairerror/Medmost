1. Scope of MEDMOST Services

MEDMOST acts solely as an independent medical travel facilitator, liaison, and logistical coordinator. MEDMOST does not own, operate, manage, or control any hospital, clinic, ambulance service, diagnostic lab, or hotel. All clinical procedures, diagnostic imaging, and treatments are carried out exclusively by accredited partner hospitals and licensed doctors in India.

2. Non-Provision of Medical Advice

Information provided on this website or during initial coordination discussions does not constitute clinical medical advice, diagnostic certainty, or a treatment prescription. Any preliminary opinions or cost estimates shared are conveyed on behalf of third-party healthcare providers and are subject to formal in-person clinical examination upon arrival in India.

3. Patient Responsibility & Informed Consent

The patient remains solely responsible for reviewing surgical risks with licensed clinicians and deciding whether to undergo any medical procedure. Treatment contracts and informed consents are signed directly between the patient and the treating hospital facility.

4. Cost Estimates and Invoicing

Preliminary treatment quotes provided by MEDMOST are approximate package estimates issued by hospitals based on remote review of submitted documents. The actual final hospital bill may vary if unexpected medical findings, comorbidities, extended ICU stays, or revised surgical protocols are determined during in-person evaluation. Medical payments are made directly to the treating hospital.

5. Travel, Passports, and Medical Visas

While MEDMOST assists in obtaining the hospital Visa Invitation Letter (VIL), the decision to grant or reject a Medical Visa (M-Visa) resides exclusively with the Government of India and the respective Indian Embassy/Consulate. MEDMOST cannot be held liable for visa delays, rejections, or border control decisions.

6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, MEDMOST, its founders, and coordinators shall not be held liable for any medical complications, surgical outcomes, medical negligence by healthcare providers, airline cancellations, or third-party service failures.
