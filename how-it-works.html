The Patient Journey, Step by Step

Traveling abroad for medical care can feel daunting. We coordinate every milestone to ensure clarity, safety, and comfort from enquiry to complete recovery.

1
Initial Step

Choose Your Treatment & Share Medical Details

Start by submitting an enquiry on our website or messaging our international patient desk via WhatsApp. Share your current diagnosis, physician notes, and recent diagnostic scans (such as MRI, CT, X-ray, or blood panels).

Recommended Documents to Prepare:

  • Recent physician consultation summary or discharge notes
  • Radiology reports and imaging files (DICOM / JPEG / PDF)
  • Current medication regimen and known allergies
  • Valid international passport copies for patient and attendant
2
Evaluation & Quotes

Receive Doctor Opinions & Hospital Quotes

We share your files with specialist departments across accredited Indian hospitals. Within 24 to 48 hours, you receive transparent feedback including:

• Doctor Preliminary Notes

Recommended surgery or medical protocol from experienced consultants.

• Estimated Hospital Cost

Package estimates covering surgery, surgeon fees, ICU, and room rent.

• Duration of Stay

Days required in hospital + post-discharge recovery before flying home.

• City & Hospital Options

Comparative choices across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, etc.

3
Visa & Travel

Visa Invitation Letter & Travel Coordination

Once you choose your hospital, we facilitate an official Visa Invitation Letter (VIL) from the hospital management. This letter allows you and up to two attendants to secure the Indian Medical Visa (M-Visa / Medical Attendant Visa) from your nearest Indian Embassy or via online e-Medical Visa portal.

Pre-Arrival Concierge: We arrange airport pickup, guide you on nearby hotel or serviced apartment reservations for your family, and coordinate a local SIM card and currency exchange.

4
Hospital Admission & Return

Hospital Admission, Treatment & Safe Return

Upon arrival at the hospital, an international patient coordinator assists with admission, translation services, pre-op tests, and physician conferences. Following procedure completion and recovery, the medical team conducts fit-to-fly assessments and provides complete discharge summaries and medication instructions.

Post-Return Telemedicine: Back home, MEDMOST helps you stay connected with your treating doctor in India for follow-up questions, lab test tracking, and telemedicine consultations.
