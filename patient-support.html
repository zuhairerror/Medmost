International Patient Support

Compassionate, comprehensive facilitation services designed to eliminate travel stress and allow patients to focus 100% on healing.

Full Concierge Care

Support Services MEDMOST Facilitates

From pre-travel paperwork to post-treatment telemedicine, explore the supportive infrastructure available to international visitors.

Medical Appointment Coordination

Navigating large medical centers in a foreign country can be overwhelming. We coordinate prioritized consultations with departmental chairs and senior specialists, ensuring your schedule is clearly blocked before you board your flight.

Includes: Pre-scheduled OPD slots, fast-tracked radiology bookings, multi-specialty tumor board coordination.

Indian Medical Visa (M-Visa) Guidance

Traveling to India for medical treatment requires a dedicated Indian Medical Visa (M-Visa). We procure official Visa Invitation Letters (VIL) on hospital letterhead with doctor signatures required by Indian embassies and consulates worldwide.

Includes: Support for Medical Attendant Visas (for accompanying relatives), FRRO extension guidance if stay is prolonged.

Airport Reception & Transfers

Arriving in a foreign airport while unwell requires extra sensitivity. Hospital representative or driver meets you at the airport arrival terminal with a personalized name-board, assisting with luggage and direct transfer to the hospital or hotel.

Includes: Air-conditioned private vehicles, wheelchair-accessible transport, and immediate medical transfer if necessary.

Accommodation & Living Guidance

While the patient is admitted to the hospital, accompanying family members need safe, hygienic, and affordable lodging. We guide you toward vetted hotels, guest houses, and serviced apartments with kitchenettes within walking distance of the hospital campus.

Options: Budget guest houses ($20-$35/night) to luxury 4-5 star hotels ($60-$120/night).

Translation & Multilingual Assistance

Communication is vital for medical safety. Indian hospital international lounges employ full-time medical interpreters fluent in Russian, Arabic, French, Uzbek, and other world languages to accompany you through every clinical interview.

Ensures: Complete understanding of surgical risks, medication schedules, and discharge recommendations.

Post-Discharge & Home Follow-up Care

Our relationship doesn't conclude when you board your flight home. We facilitate digital follow-up consultations with your treating Indian specialist, help clarify prescription questions, and coordinate report reviews over secure telemedicine channels.

Benefits: Peace of mind during long-term rehabilitation and local doctor coordination.
Service Terms & Availability:

Actual support services depend on availability, flight arrival schedules, and confirmed arrangements with the hospital's international patient desk. Certain third-party services (such as hotel reservations or private city transportation) are payable directly by the patient or companion.
