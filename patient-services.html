Facilitation Support We Provide

Navigating overseas medical treatments requires careful coordination. Here is how MEDMOST supports international travelers.

Hospital & Doctor Enquiry Assistance

We review your medical documentation, diagnostic imaging, and physician summaries to identify appropriate healthcare departments across accredited Indian hospitals. We facilitate preliminary medical opinions so you can compare hospital options before traveling.

Includes: Medical records translation, pre-travel doctor case reviews, preliminary procedure quotes.

Appointment Coordination

Avoid lengthy hospital queues. We arrange prioritized appointments with senior consultants, diagnostic scheduling, and direct hospital admission timelines prior to your flight departure.

Includes: Priority OPD scheduling, expedited lab/scan appointments, and surgical theater booking assistance.

Travel Planning & Medical Visa (M-Visa) Guidance

Traveling to India for healthcare requires an Indian Medical Visa (M-Visa). We secure official hospital Visa Invitation Letters (VIL) required by Indian embassies and online e-Medical Visa portals for both the patient and accompanying family members.

Includes: Official hospital letterhead VILs, flight timing suggestions, and FRRO registration guidance.

Accommodation Assistance

While the patient receives inpatient hospital care, accompanying family members require hygienic, comfortable, and affordable lodging. We guide you toward vetted guest houses, serviced apartments with private kitchens, and partner hotels near the hospital.

Options: Budget rooms ($20–$35/night) to premium serviced apartments and hotels ($50–$120/night).

Airport Pickup Coordination

Arriving in a foreign country when unwell requires careful handling. A driver or hospital coordinator meets you directly at the international arrival terminal with a personalized placard, assisting with luggage and direct transfer to your hotel or hospital room.

Includes: Air-conditioned private cars, wheelchair assistance, and emergency ambulance reception if required.

Language & Communication Support

Clear communication between patient and doctor is vital. Major Indian hospitals feature dedicated international patient lounges with certified interpreters for Russian, Arabic, French, and other languages to ensure full clinical comprehension.

Ensures: Transparent explanations of surgical risks, medication schedules, and written discharge instructions.
Service Availability Notice:

The actual availability of these services depends on confirmed arrangements and mutual agreements with the treating hospital facility for each individual patient. Third-party travel expenses (such as hotel accommodation and international flights) are payable directly by the patient.
