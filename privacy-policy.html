Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How MEDMOST handles patient enquiries, diagnostic files, and international patient confidentiality.

Effective Date: October 2026 (Version 1.0)

1. Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

At MEDMOST, we recognize that health information is deeply personal and sensitive. We are dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data protection and privacy when international patients contact us to explore medical treatment in India.

2. First Version Website Demonstration Notice

Please note that this initial version of the MEDMOST website is a design demonstration prototype. Contact forms on this prototype do not store or persist identifiable health records in an external database. All submitted demonstration data remains local to your browser session.

3. Information We Collect During Operational Service

When engaging with our coordination desk via email, WhatsApp, or official enquiry portals, we may collect:

  • Contact details (name, email address, country of residence, WhatsApp or telephone number).
  • Medical records voluntarily submitted (diagnostic summaries, lab tests, radiology scans, doctor prescriptions).
  • Travel preferences (target dates, companion count, special mobility or dietary requests).

4. How Your Information is Used

Your medical records are used exclusively to facilitate opinions and cost quotes from accredited hospitals and licensed medical professionals in India. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or publicly share patient details with marketing agencies or unrelated third parties.

5. Sharing with Accredited Hospital Partners

With your explicit consent, your medical files are encrypted and transferred to the international patient departments of hospitals you have expressed interest in, enabling clinical specialists to formulate preliminary treatment plans.

6. Data Retention and Erasure

Patients have the right to request full erasure of their contact information and submitted diagnostic records from our files at any time by contacting our coordination desk.

7. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For privacy concerns or to withdraw medical file sharing, please reach out via our contact channels or phone placeholder (+7 960 1726800999).
